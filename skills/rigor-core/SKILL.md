# RIGOR core protocol

## Activation

Use only after the question is classified Level 3, 4, or 5. If not activated, explain why RIGOR is disproportionate. Never lower an assigned level without a written rationale.

## Control flow

1. Orchestrator creates the brief, master plan, hypothesis register, frontier, and finite loop policy.
2. Reconcile a host-native plan before execution.
3. Activate discovery, verification, and then an independent challenge pass.
4. Activate specialist auditors only for their evidence regime.
5. Update hypotheses and convert discriminating next steps into prioritized frontier tasks.
6. Run another bounded iteration only while a material task remains inside the approved scope, cost, permissions, and loop limit.
7. Synthesizer uses only ledger-backed claims and records the frontier outcome.
8. Require a human owner before external or high-stakes consequence.

## Role contract

Each role receives brief, master plan, permitted tools/data, bundle state, and stop condition. It returns an HO handoff: work performed, deviations, claim IDs, source IDs, gaps, limitations, confidence rationale, and recommended next action.

## Invariants

- Model output is unverified until grounded in a source record.
- Material claims cite SR sources that resolve within the bundle.
- Discovery never validates itself; synthesis never invents evidence; challenge is independent.
- A hypothesis is a testable working explanation, never evidence or a disguised claim. Expected traces are not evidence.
- Missing evidence weakens a hypothesis only after coverage, survival, access, and search adequacy are assessed.
- A new iteration is not revalidation; revalidation happens after an accepted conclusion under recorded triggers.
- Do not publish, contact, accuse, purchase, trade, or make a binding decision without authorized human approval.
- Output conforms to schemas/research-bundle.schema.json.
