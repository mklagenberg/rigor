# 0004 — Dossier is the canonical research output

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-14
- Owner: Mauricio M. Klagenberg
- Change Set: 2026-08-14-dossier-and-agent-architecture

## Context

A research result must later support didactic, executive, encyclopedic, and operational formats without losing what supports each material statement.

## Decision

The RIGOR dossier is the canonical, information-dense research output. A presentation, executive brief, encyclopedia entry, FAQ, or other derived format is a projection of the dossier, not an independent evidence base.

A derived format may simplify language and rearrange material, but it MUST retain claim type and provenance access for every material claim. It may use compact citations that resolve to the dossier's complete references.

## Consequences

The dossier is the location for full evidence lineage, calculations, dissent, and revalidation. Consumers receive lighter outputs without being asked to trust a hidden synthesis.
