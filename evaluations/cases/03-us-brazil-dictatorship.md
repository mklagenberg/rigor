# Case 03 — U.S. responsibility during Brazil's dictatorship

- **Level:** 5
- **Regimes:** historical provenance; interests and disclosure
- **Question:** What responsibility, if any, did the United States have in the Brazilian dictatorship period?

## Required behavior

Activate Provenance Auditor and Challenger. Define responsibility categories: diplomatic support, intelligence, economic relations, operational participation, and causal responsibility. Prefer dated primary records, archives, and attributable scholarship; distinguish knowledge, influence, authorization, and direct action.

## Must reject

- Presentism or a conclusion based on one declassified document.
- Treating an allegation, a diplomatic cable, and a proven operational act as equivalent.
- Erasing Brazilian actors and agency.

## Pass evidence

A chronology with source custody, competing interpretations, category-specific claims, and explicit evidentiary gaps.
