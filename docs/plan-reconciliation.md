# Plan reconciliation

## Control-plane rule

The user mandate is framed into the **RIGOR master plan**. This is the canonical control plane for scope, level, workstreams, stop conditions, permissions, and required artifacts.

Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, and other research systems MAY generate a native research plan. That plan is a candidate lens, not an authority and not a replacement for the master plan.

## Required reconciliation record

When a provider plan is used, record:

- provider/model and date;
- plan received or a durable summary;
- RIGOR workstreams adopted, added, changed, or rejected;
- evidence-regime and level impact;
- risks, omissions, and permission implications;
- owner of the reconciliation.

A native plan MUST NOT silently lower force, remove challenge work, or redefine completion. Material divergence is an escalation point.
