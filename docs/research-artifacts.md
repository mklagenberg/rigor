# Research artifacts

RIGOR's canonical artifact is the [dossier](dossier-and-derivations.md). These are its minimum registers.

## Investigation brief

```text
Question:
Decision / learning outcome:
Scope and exclusions:
Period and jurisdiction:
Level and rationale:
Evidence regimes:
Stakes if wrong:
Constraints and permissions:
Known claims / hypotheses:
Human decision owner:
```

## Research plan and reconciliation

```text
workstream | question | evidence target | discovery route | role/owner |
level gate | stop condition | output | challenge route
provider/model | adopted/changed/rejected | reconciliation rationale
```

## Source and reference register

```text
source_id | author/organization | title | type | venue/place | published | accessed |
origin | locator | independence_group | status | incentives | limitations
```

## Evidence ledger

```text
claim_id | claim_type | statement | citations | evidence_lineage | support | conflict |
confidence | assumptions | unresolved_questions
```
