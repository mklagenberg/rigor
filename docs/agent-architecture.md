# Agent architecture

RIGOR agents are **roles with contracts**, not a fixed cast. The orchestrator activates them according to level, evidence regime, permissions, and expected value of independent review.

## Kernel roles

| Role | Primary responsibility | May not do |
|---|---|---|
| Orchestrator | maintain brief, master plan, gates, assignments, and escalation | silently change level or approve external action |
| Source discovery | locate primary sources, authorities, specialists, communities, and counter-sources | validate its own discoveries |
| Evidence verifier / librarian | normalize references, trace lineage, assess support and independence | promote a source claim to fact without evidence |
| Challenger | seek falsifiers, alternatives, missing lenses, and misleading framing | suppress a supported conclusion |
| Hypothesis and frontier controller | maintain hypotheses, expected traces, task priority, loop policy, and termination state | treat an expected trace as evidence or continue a loop without material task value |
| Dossier synthesizer | compose only ledger-backed claims and preserve uncertainty | invent evidence or erase dissent |

## Activated specialist roles

- **Scientific method auditor** — for critical scientific or medical claims; audits design, execution, analysis, reporting, and applicability.
- **Quantitative auditor** — for material metrics, datasets, charts, or benchmarks; audits definitions, denominators, transformations, and alternative lenses.
- **Historical / genealogical provenance auditor** — for historical responsibility, archive, origin, and lineage claims.
- **Interests and disclosure analyst** — maps explicit and non-obvious interests, incentives, ownership, funding, and framing pressures.
- **Revalidation sentinel** — monitors declared triggers after a volatile dossier is accepted.
- **Forecast and calibration auditor** — audits base rates, assumptions, forecast ranges, backtesting, and post-horizon scoring when a prospective claim is material.

## Standard handoff

Each role returns: work performed; deviations from plan; sources and references; claims and evidence status; linked hypotheses/frontier tasks where applicable; limitations; confidence rationale; unresolved gaps; recommended escalation or next action.

No role may publish, contact, accuse, purchase, trade, or make a binding decision without explicitly authorized human approval.
