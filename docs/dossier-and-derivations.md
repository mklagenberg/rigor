# Dossier and derivations

## Canonical artifact

For every completed RIGOR investigation, the **dossier** is the primary artifact. It is deliberately dense and sufficient to derive a presentation, executive brief, encyclopedia entry, FAQ, or other reader-oriented format later.

A dossier MUST contain:

1. brief, scope, level, permissions, and decision owner;
2. canonical research plan and any plan-reconciliation record;
3. claim map and typed claim ledger;
4. source register, full references, and citation links;
5. evidence lineage, methods, calculations, and data definitions;
6. incentives and interests map where material;
7. counterevidence, challenge results, dissent, limitations, and uncertainty;
8. conclusion, confidence rationale, and revalidation triggers;
9. hypothesis register, research frontier, loop policy, iteration journal, and frontier outcome;
10. appendices for searches, extracts, and reproducibility material where feasible.

## Derived formats

A derivative MUST identify the dossier it derives from. It MAY summarize, reorder, translate, or make content audience-appropriate. It MUST NOT turn an inference into a fact, hide material qualification, or sever a material claim from its citation and complete reference.

The dossier remains the source of truth when a derivative conflicts with it.
