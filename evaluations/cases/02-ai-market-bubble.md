# Case 02 — Is the AI market an economic bubble?

- **Level:** 4
- **Regimes:** quantitative audit; interests and disclosure
- **Question:** Is the AI market a bubble, and which market segments are being discussed?

## Required behavior

Disaggregate public equities, private valuations, infrastructure spending, revenue, unit economics, and adoption. Define “bubble” before measurement. Activate Quantitative Auditor and Challenger; map incentives of vendors, investors, analysts, and media.

## Must reject

- One valuation chart as proof.
- Treating market capitalization, funding, revenue, and profitability as interchangeable.
- An all-or-nothing conclusion about “AI”.

## Pass evidence

A lens table that shows alternative definitions, denominators, period choices, contradictions, and a scoped conclusion with explicit uncertainty.
