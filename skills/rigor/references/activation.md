# Activation and research force

Choose effort by the expected cost of being wrong and the difficulty of proving the claim.

| Level | Typical request | RIGOR behavior |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Conversation or transformation with no research | Do not activate |
| 1 | Stable fact or direct explanation | Do not activate |
| 2 | Bounded lookup or simple synthesis | Do not activate |
| 3 | Multi-source question with nontrivial scope | Structured RIGOR |
| 4 | Contested, consequential, volatile, quantitative, or incentive-laden | Corroborated RIGOR |
| 5 | High-consequence, adversarial, opaque, identity-sensitive, or plausibly deceptive | Adversarial RIGOR |

## Escalation triggers

Escalate when one or more of these becomes material:

- the claim changes meaning with denominator, baseline, period, jurisdiction, category, or missing-data rule;
- sources repeat one upstream record or depend on the same dataset;
- relevant actors have financial, political, reputational, institutional, or personal interests;
- credible experts or primary records materially conflict;
- a scientific conclusion depends on study design, endpoints, statistical choices, exclusions, or selective publication;
- identity, genealogy, historical responsibility, medical, legal, financial, safety, rights, or reputation consequences are involved;
- evidence is volatile or likely to change before use;
- manipulation, undisclosed sponsorship, fabricated provenance, or adversarial framing is plausible.

Do not de-escalate merely because time, context window, budget, or source access is limited. Record the limitation and narrow the claim or return a non-conclusion.

## Level controls

| Control | L3 | L4 | L5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Brief, plan, source register, claim ledger | Required | Required | Required |
| Independent corroboration | Recommended | Required or exception | Required or stop |
| Counterevidence search | Recommended | Required | Required and adversarial |
| Interest/incentive mapping | When material | Required when material | Required |
| Specialist audit | When triggered | Required when triggered | Required when triggered |
| Independent challenge role | If uncertainty remains | Required | Required |
| Revalidation plan | When volatile | Required | Required with owner |
| Human decision gate | External action | Material action | Consequential use |
