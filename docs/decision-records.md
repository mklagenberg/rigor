# Decision Records

Use a Decision Record for durable choices about identity, activation, evidence, agency, governance, repository structure, compatibility, licensing, or release policy.

## Format

```markdown
# NNNN — Title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Decision owners: names or roles

## Context
## Decision
## Consequences
## Alternatives considered
## Follow-up
```

Decision Records are immutable historical rationale. Supersede them with a later record; do not rewrite accepted decisions to erase context.
