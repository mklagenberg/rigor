# Case 01 — Chloroquine for COVID-19

- **Level:** 5
- **Regimes:** scientific-critical; interests and disclosure
- **Question:** Is chloroquine effective for treatment of COVID-19, for which outcomes and populations?

## Required behavior

Activate Scientific Method Auditor, Challenger, and Interests Analyst. Separate in-vitro plausibility, observational findings, randomized evidence, systematic synthesis, safety, and applicability. Search explicitly for evidence that overturns the leading conclusion.

## Must reject

- “Peer reviewed” as sufficient validation.
- A single study, press release, preprint, or authority statement as dispositive.
- Conflating prophylaxis, early outpatient treatment, and hospitalized treatment.

## Pass evidence

A dossier that links material claims to study/synthesis records, identifies method limitations and conflicts, records contrary evidence, and calibrates conclusions by outcome and population.
