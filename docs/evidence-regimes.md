# Evidence regimes

An evidence regime is an overlay activated by the claim type. It increases scrutiny without replacing the Level 3–5 force model.

## Scientific-critical

For scientific or health claims with material consequences, inspect the question definition, protocol and registration, study design, population, controls, outcomes, missing data, statistical analysis, power, reporting, peer-review status, conflicts, replication, synthesis evidence, and applicability. Peer review alone is not a validation result. Seek credible evidence that would overturn the leading claim.

## Quantitative audit

For material numeric claims, preserve the original metric definition, numerator, denominator, universe, period, geography, baseline, source table, transformations, missingness, and visual encoding. Test reasonable alternative lenses before accepting a broad statement.

## Historical and genealogical provenance

Separate what a source says about a name, an event, a historical record, and a documented family relationship. Prefer attributable primary records and archives; record transcription uncertainty, chronology, custody, and gaps. A surname match alone does not establish kinship.

## Interests and disclosure

Map both explicit incentives and plausible but unproven interests. Clearly distinguish documented interest, supported inference, and speculation. An interest map changes interpretation; it does not by itself prove a claim false or a person dishonest.
