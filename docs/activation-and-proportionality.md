# Activation and proportionality

## Why RIGOR has a potentiometer

Research effort should follow the expected cost of being wrong, not a taste for exhaustive work. RIGOR therefore uses levels instead of one universal procedure.

## Levels

| Level | Typical work | RIGOR status |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Direct explanation from stable knowledge | Do not use |
| 1 | Simple retrieval of an official feature, policy, or fact | Do not use |
| 2 | Bounded synthesis or comparison with ordinary uncertainty | Do not use |
| 3 | Multi-source inquiry with nontrivial scope or interdependencies | Use structured RIGOR |
| 4 | Contested, consequential, volatile, or incentive-laden question | Use corroborated RIGOR |
| 5 | High-consequence, adversarial, opaque, or potentially deceptive environment | Use adversarial RIGOR |

A simple question can rise to Level 3 if an authoritative source conflicts with primary evidence, a decision depends on the answer, or the claim is unusually volatile.

## Level selection factors

Score each factor as low, medium, or high:

- consequence of error;
- reversibility of the decision;
- volatility of relevant facts;
- evidence opacity or access asymmetry;
- disagreement or competing narratives;
- incentive and manipulation risk;
- number of dependent subquestions;
- rights, safety, financial, legal, or reputational exposure.

A high rating in consequence, rights/safety exposure, or deception risk is enough to require Level 4 review. Level 5 requires an explicit human owner.

## Minimum force by level

| Control | L3 | L4 | L5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Brief, plan, source register | Required | Required | Required |
| Primary-source pursuit | Expected | Required for material claims | Required with gap log |
| Independent corroboration | Recommended | Required or exception | Required or escalation |
| Counterevidence search | Recommended | Required | Required and adversarial |
| Incentive / ownership analysis | When relevant | Required when material | Required |
| Multiple model/workstream challenge | Optional | Recommended | Required when tooling permits |
| Human approval | If external action | Required for material action | Required before conclusion is acted on |
| Revalidation plan | When volatile | Required | Required with owner and cadence |

## Escalation and de-escalation

Escalate immediately when new evidence increases stakes, exposes a material conflict, invalidates a source lineage, or reveals active manipulation. De-escalate only after recording why the higher-level trigger no longer applies; lack of time or budget is not a de-escalation rationale.
