# Activation and proportionality

## Why RIGOR has a potentiometer

Research effort should follow the expected cost of being wrong, not a taste for exhaustive work. RIGOR uses Levels 3–5 rather than one universal procedure.

| Level | Typical work | RIGOR status |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Direct explanation, simple retrieval, bounded synthesis | Do not use |
| 3 | Multi-source inquiry with nontrivial scope | Structured RIGOR |
| 4 | Contested, consequential, volatile, or incentive-laden question | Corroborated RIGOR |
| 5 | High-consequence, adversarial, opaque, or potentially deceptive environment | Adversarial RIGOR |

Escalate when new evidence increases stakes, exposes a conflict, invalidates lineage, reveals active manipulation, or activates a claim-specific [evidence regime](evidence-regimes.md). De-escalate only with a recorded rationale; lack of time or budget is not enough.

| Control | L3 | L4 | L5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Brief, plan, source register | Required | Required | Required |
| Independent corroboration | Recommended | Required or exception | Required or escalation |
| Counterevidence search | Recommended | Required | Required and adversarial |
| Interests / ownership analysis | When material | Required when material | Required |
| Specialist regime auditor | When triggered | Required when triggered | Required when triggered |
| Human approval | If external action | Required for material action | Required before consequential use |
| Revalidation plan | When volatile | Required | Required with owner and cadence |
