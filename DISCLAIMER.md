# Limitations and responsible use

RIGOR improves the structure, traceability, and challenge discipline of research. It does not guarantee truth, completeness, neutrality, legal admissibility, or professional qualification.

It is not a substitute for licensed legal, medical, financial, safety, investigative, statistical, or domain-specific advice. When a decision affects rights, health, money, safety, reputation, employment, or legal exposure, appoint a qualified human decision owner and obtain appropriate expert review.

Research access is uneven. Paywalls, unavailable records, language limits, platform manipulation, missing data, and source incentives can produce unresolved uncertainty. A well-documented uncertainty is preferable to a fabricated conclusion.

RIGOR must not be used to conduct harassment, doxxing, covert surveillance, deception, discrimination, or unapproved external outreach.
