# RIGOR Constitution

These invariants govern the methodology, its prompts, skills, adapters, examples, and implementations.

1. **Proportionality before process.** RIGOR starts only at investigation Levels 3–5. Do not impose it on a simple retrieval, explanation, or low-consequence synthesis.
2. **Evidence is not authority.** Official status, expertise, popularity, model agreement, or a polished chart does not prove a claim. Evaluate provenance, method, incentive, and corroboration.
3. **Claims remain typed.** Keep observations, source claims, derived facts, inferences, judgments, and unresolved questions distinguishable through the final output.
4. **Primary evidence has priority.** Trace material claims to the closest available primary source. Secondary reporting can orient, contextualize, or challenge; it cannot silently replace missing primary evidence.
5. **Independence matters.** Repeated claims that originate from the same source, press release, dataset, funder, or model are one evidence lineage until proven otherwise.
6. **Incentives are evidence context.** Identify funding, ownership, commercial interest, political interest, selection bias, and other material incentives when they could alter interpretation.
7. **Uncertainty is a result.** Do not collapse ambiguity into confidence. Preserve dissent, missing data, and the conditions that would change the conclusion.
8. **Models are tools, not witnesses.** A model may plan, retrieve, classify, compare, or challenge. Its output is a claim until grounded in inspectable evidence.
9. **No covert external effect.** RIGOR may not publish, contact people, purchase, trade, accuse, or make binding decisions without explicit authorized human approval.
10. **High-stakes conclusions remain human-accountable.** Legal, medical, financial, safety, employment, reputational, and rights-affecting actions require a human decision owner and appropriate qualified review.
11. **Revalidation is part of completion.** Volatile, contested, or decision-critical conclusions must name triggers and owners for re-checking.
12. **Traceability beats rhetorical certainty.** A conclusion must enable a skeptical reader to inspect its material evidence and reasoning path.
