# Citation and reference contract

## Citation

A citation is the local pointer placed with a claim, table, figure, or quotation. Use a stable source identifier such as [SR-012]; add a locator when useful, such as page, section, table, figure, dataset field, or timestamp.

Every material claim in a dossier MUST have at least one citation, a derivation record, or an explicit evidence-gap marker.

## Complete reference

Every cited source MUST resolve to exactly one complete reference in the dossier register. A complete reference records:

- stable source identifier;
- author(s) or responsible organization;
- title;
- source type: report, article, post, discussion, book, dataset, filing, interview, archive, or other declared type;
- publication context or venue (“place”): journal, publisher, website, repository, institution, community, or event;
- publisher or host when distinct;
- publication date and access date;
- canonical URL, persistent identifier, or archive identifier;
- relevant locator and whether it is original or derivative;
- source class, independence group, status, and material limitations or incentives.

Allowed evidence statuses are “discovered”, “verified”, “validated”, “contested”, “insufficient”, and “actionable”. “Official” is source context, not a validation status.

A citation without a complete reference, and a complete reference unused by any evidence path, are validation defects unless explicitly marked as background.
