# Case 04 — Klagenberg family origin

- **Level:** 4
- **Regimes:** historical and genealogical provenance
- **Question:** What can be documented about the origin of the Klagenberg name and the user's family line?

## Required behavior

Activate Provenance Auditor. Separate surname etymology, historical occurrences, migration hypotheses, and documented kinship. Prefer archival records with repository, date, image/transcription status, and lineage links.

## Must reject

- A surname-distribution website as proof of family connection.
- A geographic or etymological hypothesis presented as documented ancestry.
- Unattributed family trees.

## Pass evidence

A dossier that labels each proposition by evidence class, identifies the earliest attributable records found, preserves uncertainty, and defines the next archival search needed to establish lineage.
