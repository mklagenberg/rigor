# 0006 — Apache-2.0 license and DCO contributions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-14
- Owner: Mauricio M. Klagenberg
- Change Set: 2026-08-14-license-and-contributions

## Decision

RIGOR is licensed under Apache License 2.0. Contributions require Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1 sign-off rather than a project-specific contributor license agreement.

## Rationale

Apache-2.0 permits broad use, modification, and distribution while providing an explicit patent license and clear redistribution conditions. DCO preserves a lightweight contribution flow while requiring contributors to certify that they have the right to submit the work.

## Consequences

- Repository distributions include the complete Apache-2.0 license.
- Every accepted commit from a contributor must carry a valid `Signed-off-by` trailer.
- Contribution review continues to require provenance, privacy, compatibility, validation, and methodology conformance checks.
- Licensing does not authorize use of third-party material beyond the rights granted by its owner.
