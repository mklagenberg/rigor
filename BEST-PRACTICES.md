# Best practices

- Start from the decision or learning outcome, not an impressive-looking search plan.
- Prefer evidence lineage over citation count.
- Search for sources that could falsify the current view before writing the conclusion.
- Keep a gap log; failed retrieval and unavailable evidence are information.
- Normalize units, definitions, periods, and populations before comparing numbers.
- Ask “who benefits if this framing is accepted?” without treating incentive as automatic proof of falsehood.
- Use one model to challenge another model’s plan, but independently verify both against sources.
- Make the final answer shorter than the research ledger; link each material conclusion to its evidence.
- Revalidate when an explicit trigger occurs instead of relying on a vague promise to check later.
