## Method and challenge

The run separated chloroquine from hydroxychloroquine, treatment from prevention, and regulatory action from efficacy evidence. It rejected peer-review status, one early study, and institutional authority alone as decisive. The challenge pass found the early evidence base heterogeneous and time-bound; it did not overturn the later synthesis-supported conclusion.
